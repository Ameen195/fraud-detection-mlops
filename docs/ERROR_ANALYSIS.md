# Error analysis (test set)

Threshold 0.0198. Fraud: 4,064; missed: 700; false alarms: 25,091. Tables are generated by `fraudguard.models.final_eval`.

## Summary

- Missed fraud is lowest-recall where the model has less to go on: `ProductCD` W (0.697 recall, 1,810 fraud), debit cards (0.762) and rows with no device info (0.756, 2,365 fraud) or no email-domain feature (0.741).
- Large payments are the weakest amount band: 0.648 recall at 1,000 and above, though that band has only 54 fraud cases. The 250-1,000 band has the best recall (0.884).
- False alarms are concentrated in `ProductCD` C (rate 0.4287), H (0.3750) and credit cards (0.3607); these also have the highest recall (0.951, 0.914, 0.913), so they are flagged more often overall.
- The biggest false-alarm band by rate is 250-1,000 (0.3702); the largest by count is `ProductCD` W (17,364) and the 100-250 band (8,004).
- "New device" and "new email domain" flags do not change recall much (0.928 vs 0.928 for device; 0.867 vs 0.842 for email), so they are not what separates caught from missed fraud here.
- No causes are tested here; these are patterns in the tables below.
